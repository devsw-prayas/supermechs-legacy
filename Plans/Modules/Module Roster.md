# Module Roster

The approved modules, with their sprite per tier and their stats.
- Stats are shown as **level 1 → max level**. Mythical has 50 levels and Ultimate has 100.
- Ultimate is the top tier (it replaces Divine). Its level-1 value is Reloaded's old Divine value.
- Heavy (gold) versions are ×1.35 on their main stats and weigh more.
- Values marked * are extrapolated (not in the Reloaded data).
- Design notes are in [[Module Decisions]].

# Energy

Tuning for all energy modules uses the percentage template (16 points, 0–8 per stat):
- **Main stat:** +1.5% per point, costs +1.25% weight.
- **Bonus stat:** costs −0.5% of the main stat and +1.25% weight.
- **Weight reduction:** −2.5% weight per point, costs −0.75% of the main stat(s).

# Resistances

- Resistance is flat: it blocks that many points of damage per hit of its element.
- High values are intended, because Ultimate weapons hit very hard (a tuned Ultimate Falcon averages about 1,525 per hit).
- Tuning uses the percentage template: resistance / HP bonus / weight reduction. Fortresses tune HP and resistance as two main stats.
- Ultimate Protectors (gold) are not in this file yet.
- Stacked resistance is part of the balance check (see [[Module Decisions]]).
