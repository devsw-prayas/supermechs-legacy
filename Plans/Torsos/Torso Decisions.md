## Brutality (`torso52`)
**Mythical → Ultimate, 3 types.** The player-facing name is **Brutality** (decided 2026-09-29); **GOD MODE is the name of the actual boss** (boss parts `torsoBoss5` / `legBoss5`, boss slot not planned yet). The art is the **lighter Reloaded twin `torso52`** (easier to recolour); the dark Legacy `torso1000` is dropped from the roster. Native red is heat; blue (energy) and yellow (physical) are new recolours *(user asked for the lighter art to colour it; confirm the extra types)*. Mythical level 1 starts at the Reloaded **Divine** stats of `torso52` (heat-heavy, as is), not the Legacy numbers. Level 50 is level 1 × the Reloaded Mythical growth (HP ×1.415, energy cap ×1.418, heat cap ×1.31). Ultimate level 1 continues from Mythical level 50 × 1.45, then ×1.63 to level 100. Energy is the mirror (caps and regen / cooling swapped), physical the average. *Levels 50 and up are derived.* A maxed Brutality mech (legs ~800, two Platinum Plating ~1,080) is ~5,500 HP. **Open:** how Brutality is obtained.
