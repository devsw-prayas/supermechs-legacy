# Flamethrower Decisions

## Flamethrowers: Overcooking Oven / Unstable Power Cell / Crimson Rapture / Ash Creator (decided 2026-09-28)
**Legendary → Mythical → Ultimate.** Two pairs, no physical version. Small look = Legendary, big look = Mythical **and Ultimate**. Reloaded names and stats plus our Ultimate rule; the Legacy names (Inferno, Lava Spray, ColdFire) are dropped. Range 1–2. They also lower the enemy's **max heat / max energy**.
- **Sharks** (Oven, Power Cell): 2 uses, cheap, **backfire**, huge heat/drain per shot ("mini EMP", kept strong on purpose, user).
- **Rapture, Ash Creator**: 3 uses, no backfire, expensive to fire.
