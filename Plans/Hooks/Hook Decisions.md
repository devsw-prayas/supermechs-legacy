# Hook Decisions

**Hooks are DONE (2026-09-27).**

## Super Grappling Hooks (decided 2026-09-27)
**Legendary → Mythical → Ultimate.** Look `D` = Legendary; look `E` = Mythical and Ultimate. One use per battle; pulls the enemy.

| Hook | Element | Legendary (`D`) | Mythical / Ultimate (`E`) | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum Grappling Hook | Physical | ![[Hooks/sprites/superHarpoonBase1D.png\|80]] | ![[Hooks/sprites/superHarpoonBase1E.png\|80]] | 17 |
| Flaming Grappling Hook | Explosive | ![[Hooks/sprites/superHarpoonBase2D.png\|80]] | ![[Hooks/sprites/superHarpoonBase2E.png\|80]] | 16 |
| Shocking Grappling Hook | Electric | ![[Hooks/sprites/superHarpoonBase3D.png\|80]] | ![[Hooks/sprites/superHarpoonBase3E.png\|80]] | 11 |

**Stats = Reloaded × 1.5** (user, 2026-09-27; hard cap: max hit ~600). Level 1 → max; Ultimate has 100 levels at Mythical's growth rate. Energy/heat costs unchanged (20 at Legendary, 31 from Mythical).

| Hook | Legendary | Mythical | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum | 100–129 → 147–189 | 152–198 → 215–281 | 231–303 → **~425–557** |
| Flaming | 78–101 → 114–147; heat dmg 29→42 | 117–153 → 167–218; heat dmg 41→59 | 179–234 → ~329–431; heat dmg 62→~113 |
| Shocking | 78–101 → 105–137; energy drain 38→51 | 117–153 → 155–203; energy drain 54→72 | 162–213 → ~266–349; energy drain 75→~123 |

**Stat damage (Mythical and Ultimate only):** permanently lowers an enemy stat for the rest of the fight, like Reloaded's heat and energy weapons. Mythical is a fixed amount; **Ultimate = Mythical × 1.8**. It does not grow with levels. Legendary hooks don't have it.

| Hook | Mythical | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|
| Shocking | −16 energy capacity, −10 energy regen | −29 energy capacity, −18 energy regen |
| Flaming | −16 heat capacity, −10 cooling | −29 heat capacity, −18 cooling |
| Platinum | none | none (see resistance damage) |

**Ultimate resistance damage (all three):** the Ultimate forms also hammer the enemy's resistance to their own element, permanently for the fight.
- Platinum → physical resistance; Flaming → explosive resistance; Shocking → electric resistance.
- **Scales with the 100 Ultimate levels: −12 at level 1 up to −35 at level 100** (about +0.23 per level; roughly −30 at level 78).
