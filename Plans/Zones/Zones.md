# Zones

Campaign map: **19 zones** (final). The 7 original zones are the base; 12 are new. Each zone: ~11 missions, 2–3 huntdowns, a boss. The main campaign is played 3 times (1v1 → 2v2 → 3v3), then Ultimate mode.

## Map layout rules
- **The boss fight of each zone sits at its right edge, on the transition to the next zone.** The boss node covers the seam between panels, so small seam or road mismatches there don't matter. (Decided 2026-09-28.)
